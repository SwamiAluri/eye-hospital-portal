How the live columns are kept (without 13 cramped fields)

You asked to keepWhere it livesWhy not its own column
Admission type (IPD / DayCare)Under the name, with UHIDRepeating it as a wide column next to every name wastes the scan line.
SurgeonUnder the procedure, and again on the railThe safety scan is eye + procedure; surgeon is the next line, always visible.
Admitted timeUnder slot (Day Care) or as the primary time (IPD)Day Care acts on slot time; IPD acts on stay. Both remain on the row.
OT stateOwn labelled columnYou said it cannot be lost. It stays a word (Admitted / In OT / Returned), never colour alone.

KPI rule (this ward, not the hospital)

Always on the board

PAC incomplete, critical lab, delayed for OT, returned from OT — counted for the selected ward. These are the misses that harm a patient this morning. “8 on today’s OT” sits in the header as context, not a sixth equal card.

Coordinator only, quieter

Financial hold and Rebook for this ward. They do not use the red treatment unless they block surgery. Hospital-wide OT today 27 / Financial 136 / Delayed 111 stay on IP census. This page will not show them.
