The safety net that makes asynchronous charge capture trustworthy. Because implants and consumables post charges through an outbox rather than synchronously, something has to prove daily that clinical use, stock movement and billed charges agree. This is that proof, plus the wastage visibility decision D2 requires.

Messages on this screen

The reader here is finance or stores, not a nurse, so numbers and event references are the content. The rule that still applies is that the headline states the money or the patient impact, and the diagnostics sit underneath it.
