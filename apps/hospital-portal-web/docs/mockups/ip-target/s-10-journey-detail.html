Closing the dual write path. handleAddTransaction on this page and in PatientQuickPanel calls ipManagementApi.addBilling, which writes ip_billing_transactions — a clinical-workflow projection, not an accounting record. Money entered here gets no receipt number, no idempotency guard, no refund or reversal rules, and never reaches the CCMS outbox.

The Audit tab is thinner than it looks. journey_audit_log is written for journey lifecycle actions plus pre-op approval and clinical-evaluation amendment. Vitals, medications, PAC, checklists, investigations and safety sign-offs produce no audit rows, so this tab cannot answer “who changed that reading”. Each history is currently reconstructed from its own table. Gap 1 in conventions.
