Decisions D1 and D4 — itemised package components and itemised consumables — make item-to-billable-variant mapping mandatory. There is currently no such mapping table, so an inventory item consumed in theatre has no price to post. This screen is where those gaps get closed before they become permanently lost revenue.

Why exceptions rather than blocking. An unmapped item never stops a surgeon, a discharge or a bill. Usage is always recorded, the charge is parked as an exception, and it replays automatically the moment the mapping exists. Clinical flow is never held hostage to a pricing gap.

Mapping actions

An unmapped item is silent revenue leakage: the device leaves the shelf, the charge never posts, and nobody notices until the margin report. These are the four ways a mapping is created or changed, plus what happens to the items that were missed while it was absent.
