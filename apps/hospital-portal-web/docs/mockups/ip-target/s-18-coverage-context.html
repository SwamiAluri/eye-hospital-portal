The coverage block embedded in the census rail, the pre-op workspace and the journey detail page. Read-only by design — IP displays coverage, CCMS owns it, and nothing in IP may mutate a coverage case. Its real job is to keep three different things from being confused with each other.

The three-way confusion this block exists to prevent. Coverage status (has the payer approved?), patient payment (has the patient paid their share?) and clinical discharge (is the patient fit to go home?) are independent. Conflating them is what produced the current panel’s “What is covered?” heading and what causes wards to hold beds waiting for a TPA.
