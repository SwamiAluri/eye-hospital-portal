Six panels between a patient arriving at the theatre door and the case closing. Every one writes a timestamp and an actor, because theatre timings are the input to utilisation reporting and to any later incident review.

Theatre is the best-audited part of the journey. Accept, start, complete, cancel, barcode verification and the WHO override all write journey_audit_log, and completion publishes SurgeryCompleted to integration_outbox_event. The gap is the safety checklist and anaesthesia readings themselves, which are not audited — the timestamps on the record are the only evidence of when a sign-off happened.
