Every ward action

Six actions reachable from a ward row. Each is a focused dialog (role="dialog", aria-modal, focus trap, Escape, focus restored). The three that are irreversible or move money route through S-17.

1Transfer bed
Transfer bed — Meena Iyer✕
CurrentDay Care · B-04
Only beds that Auth reports as Available are listed. IP requests the transition; BedLifecyclePolicy decides.
CancelTransfer

Vacate and occupy are one atomic request. A partial transfer leaves two beds wrong at once.

2Record vitals
Record observation — Meena Iyer✕
Out-of-range values are flagged on save and surface on the ward board as a deteriorating-patient chip. They are never silently accepted.
CancelSave observation

The highest-frequency ward action, so it is two clicks from the board and never leaves the page.

3Cancel today’s surgery
Cancel today’s surgery✕
The patient stays admitted. Only the scheduled surgery is cancelled; slot AM-3 is released back to the OT board.
  • ✓ Bed B-04 retained
  • ✓ Slot AM-3 released
  • ✓ Advance retained, no refund triggered
Keep scheduledCancel surgery

Listing the consequences prevents the most common confusion: cancelling the surgery is not cancelling the admission.

4Cancel admission
Cancel admission✕
This releases bed B-04 and ends the journey. It cannot be undone.
  • ✓ Bed B-04 released to inventory
  • ✓ Slot AM-3 released
  • ✕ Advance ₹30,000 not refunded automatically
A refund is a separate authorised action in S-12. The patient appears on the Cancelled tab with “Refund advance” as the next action so the money is not forgotten.
Keep admissionCancel admission

Destructive and money-adjacent, so it uses the S-17 pattern: consequences named, reason mandatory, confirm disabled until valid.

5Rebook cancelled surgery
Rebook surgery — Farid Khan✕
Cancelled 24 Aug — BP 190/110 on arrival. Pre-op steps already completed are retained; consent and PAC are re-validated for the new date.
  • ✓ Investigations still valid (within 7 days)
  • ✕ PAC must be repeated
  • ✕ Consent date must be updated
CancelRebook

Rebooking states which clearances survive and which expire. Silently carrying forward a stale PAC is a patient-safety issue, not a convenience.

6Send to OT

  • ✓ Site marked — OS (left)
  • ✓ Consent in file
  • ✓ Fasting confirmed
The bed stays occupied while the patient is in theatre. Releasing it here is how a day-care patient returns to a bed that has been given away.
CancelSend to OT

The ward-side half of the handover. The OT-side acceptance is on S-06; both halves are recorded.
