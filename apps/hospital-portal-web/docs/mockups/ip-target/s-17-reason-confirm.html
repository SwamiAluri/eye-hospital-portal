One shared dialog for every action that reverses a clinical state, overrides a gate, releases a bed or moves money backwards. It replaces six bespoke inline forms that each ask for a reason slightly differently, and it guarantees that no such action reaches the server without an audited reason.

Contract. Props: title · consequence sentence · minimum reason length · destructive flag · confirm label. Every consumer writes a journey_audit_log row carrying the reason, the actor and a correlation id. The confirm button stays disabled until the reason meets the minimum length.

Two consumers of this dialog write no audit row at all. Revoking a consent and rejecting a department clearance are exactly the kind of decisions a reason box exists to capture, yet the reason currently lands only on the record itself and never in journey_audit_log. Collecting a justification and then not storing it where anyone would look is worse than not asking.
