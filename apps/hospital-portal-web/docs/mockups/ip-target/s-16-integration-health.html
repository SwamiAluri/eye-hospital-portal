Data & action map

There are two independent outboxes, not one. IP Management owns integration_outbox_event and publishes only JourneyAdmitted, SurgeryCompleted, PatientClinicallyDischarged and EmergencyFcApplied. Auth owns billing_ccms_outbox and publishes BillFinalized, PaymentReceived, PaymentReversed and PatientObligationChanged. They have different processors, different retry behaviour and different status vocabularies, which is precisely why one screen has to show both.
