  • 2Staggered arrival times are the point. Batching by session and sequencing within it is what converts theatre capacity into throughput without a waiting-room queue.
  • 2Readiness is shown at planning time, not discovered on the morning. A blocked slot at 16:00 the day before is a problem that can still be solved.

Data & action map

This screen is built on nothing that exists yet. There is no slot function class, no journey_slot table and no slot route anywhere in ip-management-service. Today the closest thing is PUT /api/ip-management/journeys/{id}/ot, which stamps theatre and timing fields directly onto patient_journey — enough to say “this patient is in theatre 1 at 09:00”, but with no sequence, no session and therefore no way to re-order a list. Every scheduling change needs to be a history, not a field. “Who moved this patient from third to seventh on the list, and when?” is the question a coordinator asks after a list overruns. That is why journey_slot is specified as an append log with a release rather than a mutable position column on the journey.
